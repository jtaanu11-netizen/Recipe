Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal.

.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
  2. Heat a pan and pour in the eggs.
  3. Cook until done and serve.
